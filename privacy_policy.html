Privacy Policy

Nod · Last updated: March 9, 2026

Nod ("the app," "we," "us") is a restaurant menu recommendation app that uses artificial intelligence to help individuals and groups choose dishes. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

Camera and Photos

When you scan a menu, you grant Nod access to take a photo or select an image from your photo library. These images are:

  • Transmitted over HTTPS to an AI processing service (OpenRouter) to extract dish names, descriptions, and prices.
  • Not stored on our servers. We do not operate any image storage infrastructure.
  • Processed ephemerally by the AI service and not retained beyond the request lifecycle per OpenRouter's data handling policies.

Preferences You Provide

Dietary restrictions (vetoes), food cravings, and mood preferences you enter within the app are stored only on your device using iOS's local UserDefaults/AppStorage mechanism. This data is never transmitted to us.

API Keys

If you choose to enter a custom AI API key in Settings, it is stored securely in iOS's AppStorage on your device only. It is transmitted only to the AI service provider (OpenRouter) when making AI requests, and is never transmitted to us.

Local Network (Peer-to-Peer Features)

The AI Group Negotiation feature uses Apple's MultipeerConnectivity framework (Bonjour) for direct device-to-device communication on your local network. No data in this process is routed through our servers. The only data exchanged between devices are:

  • Anonymized menu data (dish names and prices from the scanned menu)
  • AI-generated negotiation messages
  • Your device's display name (as set in iOS Settings → General → About)

This communication is limited to devices on the same local Wi-Fi or Bluetooth network and does not require an internet connection.

2. Third-Party Services

OpenRouter (AI Processing)
Menu images and text prompts are sent to OpenRouter (openrouter.ai/privacy) for AI inference. OpenRouter routes requests to underlying AI model providers (e.g., Google Gemini). Please review OpenRouter's privacy policy for details on how inference data is handled by their service and upstream providers.

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any account information.
  • We do not use analytics, crash reporting SDKs, or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not track your location.
  • We do not build user profiles.
  • We do not sell data to third parties.

4. Children's Privacy

Nod is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will address it promptly.

5. Data Retention

Because we do not operate servers that store user data, there is no user data for us to retain or delete. All preferences stored on your device can be cleared by deleting the app.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be noted with an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the App Store support link on the Nod product page.
